5-Day Japan Trip Plan

Day 1: Arrival in Tokyo and Exploring Shinjuku

  • Morning: Arrive at Narita or Haneda airport and take a train or bus to your hotel in Shinjuku
  • Afternoon: Visit the Tokyo Metropolitan Government Building for a free observation deck with great city views
  • Evening: Explore the Shinjuku Gyoen National Garden and enjoy a traditional Japanese dinner at a local restaurant

Day 2: Tokyo Landmarks

  • Morning: Visit the famous Tsukiji Outer Market for a sushi breakfast and fresh seafood
  • Afternoon: Take a stroll in the Imperial Palace East Garden and visit the Meiji Shrine
  • Evening: Enjoy a traditional Japanese tea ceremony and explore the trendy Harajuku district

Day 3: Pop Culture and Entertainment

  • Morning: Visit the Ghibli Museum, dedicated to the works of Studio Ghibli and its founder Hayao Miyazaki
  • Afternoon: Explore the colorful streets of Akihabara, Tokyo's electronic and anime hub
  • Evening: Enjoy a sumo wrestling tournament at the Ryogoku Kokugikan or a traditional Japanese theater performance

Day 4: Day Trip to Nikko

  • Morning: Take a day trip to Nikko, a UNESCO World Heritage site located about two hours away from Tokyo by train
  • Afternoon: Visit the Toshogu Shrine, a lavish Shinto shrine dedicated to Tokugawa Ieyasu, and the Rinno-ji Temple
  • Evening: Return to Tokyo and enjoy a traditional Japanese dinner and relax at a local onsen (hot spring)

Day 5: Modern Tokyo

  • Morning: Visit the teamLab Borderless digital art museum in Odaiba
  • Afternoon: Explore the futuristic district of Shibuya, including the famous Shibuya Crossing and Hachiko statue
  • Evening: Enjoy a farewell dinner at a Michelin-starred restaurant and depart from Tokyo
